SWOOP STUDIO 0.2.5

INSTALL
Download the Windows x64 installer from:
https://github.com/psigho/swoop-studio/releases/latest

Run Setup, then open Swoop Studio from the Start menu or Desktop shortcut.
Windows 10/11 on an Intel/AMD 64-bit PC is required. The installer is unsigned.
Node.js, npm and a separate FFmpeg installation are not needed.

TRY IT
Play the editable playground, or choose Help > Watch included demo.
Record opens the Windows screen/window picker. Import video opens your own clip.
Use Cursor for shapes, trails, click effects and drag effects; Motion for zooms.

KEEP YOUR WORK
Recordings are backed up to disk while capture runs. Use Recordings to reopen a
take or show its folder. Completed takes include cursor data. Interrupted takes
may have a missing ending or cursor track. Backups never expire automatically.
Save project keeps the source video and your edit in a .swoop file.
Export creates the finished WebM or MP4 video. Leave the app open until it ends.
